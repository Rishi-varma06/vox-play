Chess commentary listening study

Participant __PARTICIPANT__ · about 25 minutes

You will listen to four short chess commentary clips and rate each one. There are no right answers — we want your impression as a listener.

Before you start

  • Taking part is voluntary. You may stop at any point, for any reason, without explaining.
  • We record only your ratings, your comments, and two background questions about chess.
  • We do not record your name, email, or anything that identifies you. Your responses carry a code only.
  • Because responses are anonymous, they cannot be withdrawn after you send them — nothing links them back to you.
  • Responses are kept until the project is marked, then deleted.

Please use headphones or speakers in a quiet place, and do not refresh the page once you begin.
